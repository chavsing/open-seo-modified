Information we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and name. If you sign up with email, we store your password in hashed form, never in plain text. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from that provider.
  • What you put into the app. The websites, keywords, competitors, prompts, reports and settings you add to your projects.
  • Google data you choose to connect. If you connect Google Search Console or Google Analytics, we read the data described in "Google user data" below.
  • Billing records. Your plan, payment dates, credit balance and the email address you paid with. Payments are handled by PayPal. We never receive or store your card or bank details.
  • Technical information. IP address, browser type and server logs, which we use to keep the service running and secure.
  • Messages you send us, such as support emails.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run the features you use: keyword research, competitor and backlink analysis, rank tracking, site audits, AI search visibility and reports.
  • To manage your plan, payments and usage credits.
  • To send emails about your account, such as email confirmation, password resets and important service notices. We may also send occasional product updates, and every one of those has an unsubscribe link.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud and automated sign-ups.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

If you connect Google Search Console or Google Analytics, you choose which Google account to connect and Google asks for your permission first. We request read-only access:

  • Search Console (webmasters.readonly): the search queries, pages, clicks, impressions and positions for the sites you choose.
  • Google Analytics (analytics.readonly): traffic and engagement figures for the properties you choose.

We use this data only to show your own search and traffic figures inside your AGS MASTER projects and reports, next to your SEO research. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to train AI models, and we do not share it with anyone except as needed to provide those features to you, to comply with the law, or to protect the security of the service. People on our side do not read it unless you ask us to for support, or the law or security requires it.

AGS MASTER's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The access Google gives us is stored encrypted. You can remove a connected Google account at any time from your project's settings, which deletes the stored access, and you can also revoke it from your Google account permissions.

Services we rely on

We share information only with the providers that run parts of the service for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, data storage and the sign-up captcha.
  • Google and Microsoft: sign-in, and the Google data you choose to connect.
  • Loops: sending account and product emails. It receives your name and email address.
  • DataForSEO: SEO data. It receives the keywords, domains and pages you research, but not your name or email address.
  • PayPal: payments.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies

We use only the cookies the service needs to work: keeping you signed in and protecting sign-up from bots. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and project data within 30 days, except billing records we must keep for tax or legal reasons. Copies in backups are removed as those backups expire.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your details in the app, and remove connected Google accounts at any time. You can also email us to get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law, and we will honour them.

Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords, encrypted storage of third-party access and access controls to protect your information. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.
