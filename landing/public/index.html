How credits work

SEO data costs money to fetch, so each search or check uses a small amount of credits based on the data it pulls. Pro adds $50 of credits every month, on the monthly and yearly plans. Lifetime comes with $500 to start. Need more? Contact us and we will add extra credits, which never expire.

Questions

Is Lifetime really a single payment?

Yes. You pay $4,799 once and keep access with no renewals. It includes $500 of credits, and you can contact us to add more whenever you need them.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. If AGS MASTER isn't right for you, email us within 30 days of a Pro, Pro Yearly or Lifetime payment and we will refund it in full through PayPal. See the Terms for the details.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time: cancel the automatic payment in your PayPal account. You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for.

What happens when I run out of credits?

On Pro monthly, your credits refill each month when your payment goes through. On Pro yearly, they refill each month of the paid year. On any plan, contact us to add extra credits.

Can I switch from Pro to Lifetime?

Yes. Email us and we will help you switch.

Can I try it first?

Every new account starts on a free plan with $1 of credits, so you can look around before you pay.

Is there an AI chatbot?

Yes. The AI Assistant is a chat inside AGS MASTER. Ask it in plain words to find keywords, check your rankings, look at a competitor or review your site, and it runs the tools for you and explains what it found. It uses your credits like the rest of the app, and it is still in beta.

Can I use it from ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes. Connect AGS MASTER to Claude, ChatGPT or Grok from Agent setup in the app, then ask about your SEO data in the AI app you already use.
